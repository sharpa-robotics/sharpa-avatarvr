**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

**Organization:** Sharpa Inc. and its affiliates (“Sharpa”, “we”, “us”, or “our”)

**Application:** **Sharpa AvatarVR** (the “App”), distributed on Meta Quest / the Meta Horizon Store

**Contact:** sales@sharpa.com

**Address:** 251 Little Falls Drive, Wilmington, County of New Castle, DE 19808

# 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy is managed by Sharpa. It explains what user data **Sharpa AvatarVR** processes, how that data is used, and how you may request deletion. This Policy applies to the App as made available on Meta Quest headsets through the Meta Horizon Store.

**Sharpa AvatarVR** is an invitation-only teleoperation companion for Sharpa’s self-developed products. It is bundled with authorized Sharpa products (the “Authorized Device”), including “Sharpa D01” robot and “Sharpa AE01” wearable exoskeleton-based data gloves, and is not a standalone consumer product. It is not publicly searchable in the Meta Horizon Store.

# 2. What Data We Collect

The App may collect and process only the following data:

## 2.1 Meta account data for invitations

To issue, verify, and administer invitations, Sharpa may process identifiers associated with a user’s Meta account, including:

- an app-scoped Meta user ID;

- limited profile fields made available by Meta, such as display name or handle; and

- invitation or access-code details needed to grant or refuse access.

## 2.2 Robot identifiers and authorized diagnostic data

When the App is used while networked, and only after you authorize the diagnostic function, the App may upload to Sharpa’s backend:

- the connected Authorized Device ID;

- the connected Authorized Device serial number (SN); and

- limited runtime error information needed to monitor faults in the VR program, such as app version, error codes, crash or exception summaries, and timestamps.

Those network requests may also generate ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, request time, and device or app user-agent, used only to deliver and debug the diagnostic channel.

If you contact Sharpa about the App, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message content, and any logs or screenshots you attach.

## 2.3 Data we do not collect

**Sharpa AvatarVR** does not collect, record, store, upload or process the operator’s body or glove motion, or the operator’s VR view. Real-time control signals and robot or simulation imagery are processed locally on the device or local network to operate the product and are not uploaded as user data. If you do not authorize diagnostic upload, the App will not send robot ID, SN, or VR-program error logs to Sharpa.

# 3. How We Use Data

We use the data described in Section 2 only to:

- administer invitations and prevent unauthorized access;

- detect, investigate, and repair VR-program errors after you authorize diagnostics;

- secure the diagnostic channel and respond to support or privacy requests; and

- comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

# 4. How Data Is Shared

We disclose data only as needed to operate the App:

- Meta, for Horizon Store, account identity, and invitation features. Meta processes that platform data under its own policies;

- Sharpa personnel who support authorized deployments, on a need-to-know basis;

- infrastructure providers that host or log the diagnostic channel on Sharpa’s behalf; and

- when required by law.

# 5. Retention

Invitation records are kept while you remain an authorized operator, then deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after access ends, unless law requires longer retention.

Robot ID, SN, and authorized diagnostic logs are kept as needed to resolve VR-program errors and will be anonymized for statistical analysis. The information will be kept for a reasonable period unless a longer period is required for an active investigation or legal obligation.

Support emails are kept as needed to complete the request. Data stored in this App can be removed by clearing App data or uninstalling the App.

# 6. Your Rights and How to Request Deletion

All users, regardless of region, may request deletion of user data that Sharpa has collected or stored in connection with **Sharpa AvatarVR**. There is no fee. Depending on where you live, you may also request access to or correction of your data, or withdraw consent for optional diagnostic upload.

Email sales@sharpa.com with the subject line “**Sharpa AvatarVR** Teleoperation Data Deletion Request”. Please include, if available, your Meta display name or app-scoped user ID, invitation code or approximate invitation date, and the Authorized Device ID and/or SN.

We will verify the request and, within 30 days or sooner if required by law, delete or anonymize Sharpa-controlled records that reasonably relate to you. Uninstalling the App removes local App data. Sharpa cannot delete Meta account data or Meta-controlled backups; please use Meta’s settings or support for those.

We may decline or limit a request if we cannot verify your identity, the data is controlled by Meta, we must retain a limited record to comply with law or investigate a security incident, or the data has already been anonymized. If we cannot fulfill a request in full, we will explain what we could not delete and why.

# 7. Security

We apply data minimization, limit access to personnel who need the data to support Sharpa deployments, and apply corresponding encryption technology on the data sent to us via email.

# 8. International Transfers

Sharpa operates in the United States, Singapore, China, and other locations. Data described in this Policy may be processed in those countries. Where required by law, we use appropriate transfer safeguards.

# 9. Children

The App is intended for authorized adult operators. It is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact sales@sharpa.com and we will delete it.

# 10. Changes

If we materially change what data we process or how we use it, we will update this Policy.
